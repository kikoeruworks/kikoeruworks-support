Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kikoeru Works ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the iOS application "Hear My Oshi" (Japanese name: 推しが聴こえる) (the "App").

In short: The App does not require an account and does not collect any personally identifiable information. Any audio file you choose to load is processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or other personally identifiable information
  • Precise location data
  • Access to your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Health or medical information
  • Tracking data used for advertising purposes

2. Information Stored On Your Device

To provide a better experience, the App stores the following preferences locally on your device only. This information is never transmitted off the device (including to our servers):

  • Your selected Sound Profile (HRTF: head-related transfer function)
  • Your upgrade purchase status (a local cache to speed up app launch)
  • Your display language setting (Japanese / English)

Uninstalling the App permanently removes this information from your device.

3. Handling of Audio Files

The App allows you to apply HRTF binaural processing to audio files you choose (mp3, wav, ZIP archives, etc.). This processing happens entirely on your device. Audio files you select are never uploaded to or stored on any external server.

4. In-App Purchases

The App may offer an optional paid upgrade that unlocks additional features (such as loading your own audio files). Payment is processed directly through Apple's App Store billing system; we do not receive or store your payment information (such as credit card numbers). Purchase status is verified through Apple's systems, and no purchase-related information is shared with any third-party service.

5. Advertising

The App does not display advertisements and does not share data with third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not disclose any information we handle to third parties, except where required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in applicable law or new features of the App. Material changes will be announced within the App.
